# Portfolio message and voice

Audience: hiring teams and commissioning clients equally. Saudi Arabia and the UAE first, then the wider GCC. This is an audience choice, not a claim about residence, travel availability, Arabic fluency or past client results.

The promise: visitors can assess Raden's filming, editing and AI filmmaking through actual projects, understand his contribution, then discuss a role or project. Work provides the evidence. Do not replace it with invented metrics, testimonials or promises of sales growth.

## Frameworks applied

- Allan Dib, The 1-Page Marketing Plan: specify the audience, message and next action. Applied here as a short path from selected work to playback, clear credits and contact. The website covers part of the marketing plan, not a complete acquisition/nurture system. Source: https://assets.successwise.com/documents/1PMP-Canvas.pdf
- Seth Godin, This Is Marketing: choose whom the work serves and earn trust through relevant evidence. Keep the GCC audience focused while giving hiring teams and project clients equally clear paths. Source: https://guykawasaki.com/seth-godin/ (interview with the author).
- Donald Miller, Building a StoryBrand: reduce the effort of understanding the offer and taking the next step. The visitor needs to assess fit, watch relevant work and make contact. Avoid manufacturing a dramatic problem/agitation narrative for a personal portfolio. Source: https://storybrand.com/downloads/your-brand-is-not-the-hero.pdf
- William Zinsser, On Writing Well: use familiar words, cut repetition and say who did what. This is the sentence-level editing lens, alongside the copywriting skill.

## Applied copy

Hero headline retained: From idea to final frame.
Hero description: I'm Raden Hanifa. I film, edit and create with AI, from commercial reels to aviation stories.
Work introduction: Commercial reels, interviews, and work made with AI.
SwiftSoft credit: AI filmmaking and editing by Raden Hanifa, based on a script from SwiftSoft for their launch.
Contact headline: Want to see more?
Contact: I have more work to share across filming, cinematography, editing, color grading and AI filmmaking.
Region: Saudi Arabia, the UAE and the wider GCC.
Primary action: Request full portfolio. Secondary: Discuss a project or role. The user confirmed that more work is available, so this primary action is purposeful.

Voice: conversational, specific and understated. Project titles name the client and the actual work. Credits distinguish concept, script, filming, editing and AI production. No "visionary", "elevate", "captivating", "seamless", "world-class" or "award-winning" claims. Keep the existing About/footer voice unless a change materially improves clarity.

Unselected headline alternatives: "Filming. Editing. AI filmmaking." is more literal but less distinctive; "Video work by Raden Hanifa." is direct but loses the breadth of the current headline. Keep the existing headline and put specificity in the description. Contact alternative "Tell me about the role or project" is appropriate for a direct email action; the existing "Request full portfolio" still accurately describes its prepared email.

Review checklist: Can someone tell what the project is? Whose script was used? What did Raden do? Does the CTA explain what happens? Are geography and availability claims supported? Does the sentence sound natural aloud?
